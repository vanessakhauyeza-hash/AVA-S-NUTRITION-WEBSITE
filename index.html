LEARN

Nutrition basics

Start with simple concepts that can support everyday food choices.

🍚

Carbohydrates

Important sources of energy. Common examples include maize, rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes and grains.

🥚

Protein

Helps build and maintain body tissues. Sources include eggs, beans, fish, meat, milk and other legumes.

🥑

Healthy fats

Fats provide energy and support important body functions. Portion size and overall dietary pattern matter.

🥬

Micronutrients

Vitamins and minerals are needed in smaller amounts but are essential for health and normal body functions.
