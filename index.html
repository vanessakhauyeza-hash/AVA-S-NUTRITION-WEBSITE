DIGITAL NUTRITION SCREENING PLATFORM

Turning nutrition screening into structured digital information.

Ava's Nutrition Hub is developing a digital nutrition screening concept that can help move nutrition assessment from fragmented paper records toward structured, searchable and actionable information.

The platform is designed around a simple workflow: capture assessment data, apply screening criteria, organise records, identify cases that may need attention, and present information through dashboards for follow-up and planning.

01
Digital assessmentCapture key screening information in a structured form.
02
Instant organisationStore records consistently so information can be retrieved and reviewed.
03
Decision supportSurface screening results and follow-up priorities for review.
04
Dashboard insightTurn collected information into useful summaries and trends.
