LEARN

Nutrition basics

Learn simple nutrition concepts that can support everyday food choices and healthier eating habits.

🍚

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are important sources of energy. Common examples include maize, rice, potatoes, sweet potatoes and other grains and roots.

🥚

Protein

Protein helps build and maintain body tissues. Sources include eggs, beans, peas, fish, meat, milk and other protein-rich foods.

🥑

Healthy fats

Fats provide energy and support important body functions. The type and amount of fat can form part of an overall healthy dietary pattern.

🥬

Micronutrients

Vitamins and minerals are needed in smaller amounts but are essential for health, growth and normal body functions.
